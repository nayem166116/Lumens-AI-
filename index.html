LUMENS AI · CALL INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM

Every call, transcribed, understood, and acted on.

Lumens listens to phone conversations the way a careful editor reads a manuscript — catching what was said, what it meant, and what should happen next.

Product overviewUpdated August 2026
01

What Lumens does

Four jobs, done on every single call, without anyone pressing record.

Capture
Every inbound and outbound call is captured at the carrier or softphone layer — no separate recording app, no missed calls.
Transcribe
Speech becomes searchable, speaker-labeled text in under 300ms of trailing latency, in 32 languages.
Understand
Intent, sentiment, and compliance language are extracted from the transcript as the call is still happening.
Act
Lumens routes, flags, or escalates the call automatically — to a queue, a supervisor, or a CRM record — based on rules your team writes once.

Why “Lumens”

A lumen is a unit of visible light. We named the product after the idea that a call, once transcribed and understood, becomes something you can finally see clearly.
