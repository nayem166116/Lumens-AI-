PRODUCT · FEATURES

Five systems, one transcript.

Each feature reads the same live transcript — nothing is bolted on after the call ends.

Feature referenceUpdated August 2026
01

Transcription engine

Speaker-separated, punctuated, and searchable — while the call is still live.

  • ✓32 languages, automatic detection mid-call
  • ✓Speaker diarization for agent vs. caller
  • ✓Custom vocabulary for product names and jargon
  • ✓Full transcript exportable as text, VTT, or JSON
02

Real-time routing & escalation

Rules read the transcript, not just the call metadata.

Write a rule once — “if the caller mentions cancellation twice, route to retention” — and Lumens applies it to every call, in every queue, the moment the words are spoken.

TriggerAction
Sentiment drops below thresholdSilent whisper alert to a supervisor
Regulated phrase detectedRequired disclosure prompt shown to agent
Caller repeats unresolved issueAuto-escalation to senior queue
03

Compliance & redaction

Sensitive data never reaches long-term storage in the clear.

Card numbers, national IDs, and other sensitive utterances are detected and redacted from both the audio and the transcript automatically, before either is written to disk.

Retention control

Set retention per line of business — 30 days for routine calls, 7 years for regulated lines — enforced automatically.

04

Analytics & QA

Score every call the way your best QA reviewer would — at full volume.

Custom scorecards apply to 100% of calls, not a 2% sample. Trends surface automatically: which openings correlate with resolution, which objections precede churn.

05

Integrations

Transcripts and structured fields land where your team already works.

CRM
Salesforce, HubSpot, and any system with a webhook.
Telephony
Twilio, RingCentral, Genesys, and most SIP trunks.
Data warehouse
Streamed to Snowflake or BigQuery in near real time.
