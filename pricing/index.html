03

Questions

Do you charge per minute?
No. Pricing is per active line per month, regardless of call volume or duration on that line.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes, upgrades apply immediately and downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
Is there a contract minimum?
Starter and Growth are month-to-month. Enterprise agreements are typically annual.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
You can export the full transcript archive for 30 days after cancellation, after which it is permanently deleted.
